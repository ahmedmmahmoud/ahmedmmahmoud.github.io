About Me

After-Sales Service Manager, Consumer Electronics & Low-Voltage Appliances

I have a long international track record in multinational, reputable companies across consumer electronics operations and low-voltage appliances: service, maintenance, customer care and support, training, stocktaking and audit, and escalated-claim support. That path runs through RAYA, Axiom Telecom, LG, and Elba Italy, across many departments and titles, up to my current role as After-Sales Service Manager at Elba Italy S.p.A. in Egypt.

My role as After-Sales Service Manager covers:

  • Managing the after-sales service department's sections, ensuring customer satisfaction with profit growth and annual budget planning.
  • Developing and implementing after-sales service policies, procedures, plans, and strategies.
  • Managing the main service center and outsourced service centers.
  • Managing the training and call center sections.
  • Managing spares warehousing and international supplier logistics.
  • Managing CRM and admin functions, plus the maintenance fleet and field technician teams.
  • Ensuring timely resolution of customer issues, warranty claims, and escalations.
  • Coordinating with other departments to support service, retail, and wholesale operations.
  • Monitoring service performance metrics and driving continuous improvement.
